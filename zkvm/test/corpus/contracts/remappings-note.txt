solc 0.8.35+commit.47b9dedd.

--evm-version paris is not optional. solc's default target is Shanghai or
later, where it emits PUSH0 (0x5f); the corpus runs at Paris, where 0x5f is
invalid and the constructor reverts. The block still executes and every root
still round-trips -- the contract simply is not there -- so this is caught by
checking the deployment receipt's status, not by checking roots.

The imports in NamespaceSpoke.sol name ./interfaces/INamespace.sol and
./libraries/MerkleTreeLib.sol upstream; here all three sit side by side, so
the two import lines are rewritten to match. MerkleTreeLib is internal-only,
so solc inlines it and nothing needs linking.
